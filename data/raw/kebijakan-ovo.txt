Validate your instructions and process transactions;

Monitoring compliance with our terms and conditions and policies.
As a payment service provider licensed and supervised by Bank Indonesia, we are required by law to implement security measures to prevent and mitigate any risks of infringement of applicable laws and/or regulations and/or rights of third parties such as; money laundering, financing of terrorism, fraudulent activities, financial crime, which may incur damages to our end users, OVO, any of our affiliates, and/or any third parties.
Our security measures involving data processing encompass the implementation of:
Anti-Money Laundering and Countering of the Financing of Terrorism, and Counter–Proliferation Financing (AML-CFT-CPF), which we always enforce by applying the know your customers / businesses principles to identify and verify our end users;
Fraud Detection System, which will monitor and supervise any activities by any accounts that we administer and other accounts interacting with our administered accounts, in relation to our services based on parameters that we set to control any anomaly, suspicious, or fraudulent activities that may hazard the security of our ecosystem; and
Fraud Handling Process, which we have in place to help any end users or third parties encountering fraudulent activities of becoming victims thereof.

OVO Data Sharing Principles
Before we disclose Personal Data, we will ensure that any third parties receiving the data, apply appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the Personal Data at the very least with the same level as what we have implemented to protect Personal Data under our controllership. We will also require them to enter into a data sharing or confidentiality agreement to ensure that your data is legally protected.
Our disclosure of Personal Data to third parties may incorporate a controller-processor relationship between us acting as the data controller of the Personal Data we disclose and the third party as the processor. In cases where we disclose Personal Data under a controller-processor relationship, we will ensure that the Personal Data is processed by the processor only for the Purposes that are relevant to the processing activities that we instruct our processor to execute. Most importantly, we will conduct a thorough due diligence in selecting processors we engage to process Personal Data on our behalf.
We may also enter into a joint controllership with any third party, in such cases we will ensure that the other controller notifies the data subjects on their role as a controller of the Personal Data, and we and the other controller will proportionally distribute any liability arising from the processing of Personal Data that we process under the joint controller arrangement.
For clarity, we will only disclose Personal Data once we can fully ensure that:
The disclosure is so required and relevant to the implementation and fulfillment of the Purposes;
We have a solid lawful ground to disclose the personal data to the third party;
The disclosure only encompasses Personal Data that we see required and relevant to the implementation and fulfillment of the Purposes; and
We have appropriate technical and organizational measures in place to protect the personal data.

Once your Personal Data is no longer necessary for the Services or Purposes, we take steps to erase, destroy, anonymise or prevent access or use except where needed for legal compliance, safety, security, fraud prevention and detection, in line with applicable laws.

We have established and implemented technical and organizational measures to protect personal data under our retention from unauthorized processing or any activities that may infringe the rights of data subjects. These measures include, but are not limited to:
ensuring our compliance with the applicable laws and regulations, particularly those related to data protection;
enforcing and adopting this Privacy Notice, and our internal data protection policies;
processing personal data only as necessary for, as relevant to, and in accordance with the stated Purposes;
applying a risk-based approach to data processing, considering the type of data and processing activities involved;

YOUR RIGHTS OVER YOUR DATA
As provided under the applicable data protection law, data subjects whose personal data we retain and process shall have the following rights:
Rights to Obtaining Information
You can obtain information about us as the data controller who processes your personal data, the lawful basis upon which we process your data, the purposes we rely on to process your data, and your rights as the data subject. By this Privacy Notice we have provided you the aforementioned information. You can also reach out to our supporting team to have further information about your data that we retain and process (e.g. accountability of any platforms, channels, or officials that we assign to collect your data). We commit to complying with the required SLA time period for fulfilling your data subject rights in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Please refer to
Section XI
for contact details.
Rights to Rectify Data
If you find that your data that we retain needs to be corrected or updated, or you find any inaccuracy on your data, you can reach out to our supporting team to request for correction or update upon your data. Before requesting for corrections or updates, you can ask the team to provide you with your data that we retain so you can confirm their accuracy.
Right to Access and Obtain Copies
At any time you can request us to provide you with data or copies of data that you have submitted to us.
Rights of Consent Revocation, Data Deletion, and Destruction
You have the right to withdraw your consent that you have given to us for processing your personal data, as well as require us to cease any processing activities that we undertake upon your data, delete and/or destruct any of your personal data that we retain from our systems.
Exercising your aforementioned rights may render us incapable of undertaking certain or the entire activities related to our services upon you, for example; after you enforce your right to revoke your consent or delete your data, we will not be able to retain your account and thus you cannot instruct us to provide our services since our services may require us to access your account for identification and verification of your data. By exercising any of rights as provided under this sub-section, you acknowledge and agree to the consequences, you shall not take us liable for any consequences thereof.
In most cases, data subjects exercise these rights when they believe that the retention or processing of their personal data is no longer relevant to the Purposes, the data controller no longer has a lawful basis for processing, the data retention period has expired, and/or there has been a data protection failure affecting the security, confidentiality, accuracy, or integrity of their personal data.
Rights to Object Automated-Decision Making or Profiling
Where any parts of our services may subject you to automated-decision making or profiling from automated processing that may raise legal obligations upon you or significantly impact your rights as our end-users, you will be facilitated to file your objection to us. We will inform you whenever we take any measures to subject you to automated-decision making or profiling, which may raise legal obligations upon you or significantly impact your rights as our end-users.
Right to Suspend or Restrict Data Processing
If you find any processing activities committed by us towards your data that are not proportional, aligned, relevant, or necessary to cater to the Purposes, or your data that we retain is inaccurate, you may require us to suspend or restrict us from continuing the processing.
Rights of Data Portability
You have the right to obtain and/or use your personal data that we retain in any format, structure, and form that are commonly used to be readable by electronic systems. You can request us to share and transfer your personal data that we retain to other data controllers provided that their electronic systems can communicate with ours securely in accordance with the applicable data protection law.
Rights of Indemnification
Any damages arising from our proven failure in protecting your data that we retain or infringement of your rights as the data subject may grant you the right to be indemnified.
Right to Object to Direct Marketing
Sometimes we may notify you through your direct contact (e.g. phone number, email) of any marketing or promotional materials (e.g. offerings, campaigns) related to our services or any of our affiliates' products. If you find them inconvenient, you may reach out to us to be opted out of such notifications by, among others (as applicable); opting to unsubscribe/turn off the marketing, promotion, or offering notifications, or reaching out to our supporting team to opt out from such notifications.
Right Exemptions
Your rights to revoke your consent, to require deletion or destruction, to object automated-decision making or profiling, and to data portability may be subject to exemption for:
National defence and security interests;
Law enforcement interests;
Public interests in the administration of the state;
Interests in the supervision of the financial services sector, monetary policy, payment systems, and financial system stability carried out in the administration of the state; or
Statistical and scientific research interests.

Prinsip-prinsip Berbagi Data OVO
Sebelum kami mengungkapkan Data Pribadi, kami akan memastikan bahwa pihak ketiga mana pun yang menerima data tersebut menerapkan langkah-langkah teknis dan organisasi yang tepat untuk melindungi Data Pribadi setidaknya pada tingkat yang sama dengan yang telah kami terapkan untuk melindungi Data Pribadi di bawah kendali kami. Kami juga akan mewajibkan mereka untuk menandatangani perjanjian berbagi data atau kerahasiaan untuk memastikan bahwa data Anda dilindungi secara hukum.
Pengungkapan Data Pribadi kami kepada pihak ketiga dapat mencakup hubungan pengontrol-pengolah antara kami yang bertindak sebagai pengontrol data dari Data Pribadi yang kami ungkapkan dan pihak ketiga sebagai pengolah. Dalam kasus di mana kami mengungkapkan Data Pribadi dalam hubungan pengontrol-pengolah, kami akan memastikan bahwa Data Pribadi diproses oleh pengolah hanya untuk Tujuan yang relevan dengan aktivitas pemrosesan yang kami instruksikan kepada pengolah kami untuk dilakukan. Yang terpenting, kami akan melakukan uji tuntas menyeluruh dalam memilih pengolah yang kami libatkan untuk memproses Data Pribadi atas nama kami.
Kami juga dapat menjalin kerja sama pengendalian data dengan pihak ketiga mana pun. Dalam hal ini, kami akan memastikan bahwa pengendali data lainnya memberitahukan subjek data tentang peran mereka sebagai pengendali Data Pribadi, dan kami serta pengendali data lainnya akan secara proporsional membagi tanggung jawab yang timbul dari pemrosesan Data Pribadi yang kami proses berdasarkan pengaturan kerja sama pengendalian data tersebut.
Untuk memperjelas, kami hanya akan mengungkapkan Data Pribadi setelah kami dapat sepenuhnya memastikan bahwa:
Pengungkapan tersebut sangat diperlukan dan relevan dengan pelaksanaan dan pemenuhan Tujuan-Tujuan tersebut;
Kami memiliki dasar hukum yang kuat untuk mengungkapkan data pribadi kepada pihak ketiga;
Pengungkapan tersebut hanya mencakup Data Pribadi yang kami anggap perlu dan relevan untuk pelaksanaan dan pemenuhan Tujuan; dan
Kami telah menerapkan langkah-langkah teknis dan organisasi yang tepat untuk melindungi data pribadi.

Setelah Data Pribadi Anda tidak lagi diperlukan untuk Layanan atau Tujuan yang diberikan, kami akan mengambil langkah-langkah untuk menghapus, memusnahkan, menganonimkan, atau mencegah akses atau penggunaan kecuali jika diperlukan untuk kepatuhan hukum, keselamatan, keamanan, pencegahan dan deteksi penipuan, sesuai dengan hukum yang berlaku.

PERLINDUNGAN DATA PRIBADI
Kami menerapkan langkah-langkah hukum, fisik, organisasi, dan teknis yang wajar untuk memastikan bahwa Data Pribadi Anda terlindungi. Integritas Data
Kami berhak untuk berasumsi dan menganggap bahwa Anda memiliki dasar hukum yang sah untuk menyerahkan data pribadi apa pun yang Anda bagikan atau transfer kepada kami. Anda membebaskan kami dari segala tanggung jawab yang timbul dari data pribadi apa pun yang Anda serahkan kepada kami tanpa dasar hukum yang sah.
Harap pastikan keabsahan, kelengkapan, dan keakuratan setiap Data Pribadi Anda yang dikirimkan kepada kami. Kami dapat memverifikasi data pribadi Anda ke Departemen Kependudukan dan Pencatatan Sipil. Pembaruan segera setelah perubahan dapat menyelamatkan Anda dari konsekuensi ketidakakuratan data, seperti; kesalahan dalam menggunakan layanan kami, karena data Anda yang tersimpan dalam sistem kami tidak lagi valid atau sama dengan data yang Anda miliki.
Sehubungan dengan keabsahan, kelengkapan, dan keakuratan Data Pribadi Anda, Anda membebaskan kami dari segala konsekuensi yang timbul dari kelalaian atau kegagalan Anda dalam menjaga atau memastikan keabsahan, kelengkapan, dan keakuratan data pribadi Anda yang Anda serahkan kepada kami.
